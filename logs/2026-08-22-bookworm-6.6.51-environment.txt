=== The important asymmetry ===
Three raspberrypi/linux `rpt-rpi-v8` kernels tested end-to-end on this same
board (6.6.51, 6.12.93, 6.18.34) -- all three fail with identical fingerprints.
One OpenWrt kernel (OpenMANET 1.8.0's 6.6.138, built from OpenWrt's linux-6.6
tree) -- passes.

The difference is NOT the kernel version. Both trees share stable-tag numbers.
The difference is the *patch stack applied on top of mainline 6.6*. OpenWrt's
tree either includes a spi-bcm2835 fix, or does not include a regression, that
raspberrypi/linux does. Bisecting inside raspberrypi/linux (rpi-6.6.y, rpi-6.12.y,
rpi-6.18.y) would identify the exact commit; a shorter path may be diffing
`drivers/spi/spi-bcm2835.c` and adjacent SPI-core files between OpenWrt's
linux-6.6 tree and raspberrypi/linux rpi-6.6.y at similar stable tags.
