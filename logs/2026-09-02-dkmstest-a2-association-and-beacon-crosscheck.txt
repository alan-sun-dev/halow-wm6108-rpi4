===== part 2: the beacon losses are mostly NOT station-local =====
Both stations are associated to the same AP (3c:1a:cc:70:3f:ca, SSID
BCM2711-57e7) and both journals cover the same wall-clock period -- A1 booted
08-26 23:28, A2 08-26 23:58. Both report `System clock synchronized: yes` with
`NTP service: active`, and at capture their clocks and the laptop's agreed to
within 2 s, most of which is ssh round-trip. Second-level alignment between the
two journals is therefore meaningful, and a +/-5 s window is generous.

Method: extract every CTRL-EVENT-BEACON-LOSS from each station with
`journalctl -b -o short-unix`, keep the integer unix seconds, and match each A1
event against the A2 set with a +/-5 s tolerance (and the reverse).

  A2 events with an A1 counterpart within +/-5 s        37   (all of them)
  A1 events with an A2 counterpart within +/-5 s        37

second. Two different chip revisions, two different kernels, two different

===== what this changes =====
The 2026-08-28 entry read the record as "beacon loss is routine and survivable,
a BURST of it is what breaks the link". Both halves need replacing:

1. Count is not the discriminator. Bursts of four and five beacon losses inside
   fifteen seconds occurred on 08-28, 08-30 and 08-31 and the association
   survived every one; the single disconnect of the quiet period arrived with
   exactly one beacon loss.

2. The discriminator that does separate them cleanly is whether the event is
   common to both stations. 37 of 37 common-mode beacon losses were harmless.
   Every beacon loss that coincided with a disconnect was one only A1 saw.

What "A1-only" is evidence of is narrower than it sounds. The two stations are
in different rooms, so an event only A1 sees may be A1's local RF environment
rather than anything about the A1 board, its driver or its kernel. What is
excluded is the AP: the AP was beaconing normally at 09-01 21:27:00, because the
A2 station a few metres from it logged nothing at all that day until 23:37. The
survey and regulatory-domain explanations were already eliminated on 2026-08-28.

NOT ESTABLISHED, and worth stating so it is not read as settled:
 - the mechanism behind an A1-local beacon loss;
 - whether the 6 disconnects with no beacon loss share a cause with the 12 that
   have one -- reason=4 locally_generated=1 covers both;
 - whether any of this is position, board, or kernel, which needs the two
   stations swapped or a third station added. Node 3 exists for exactly this.
